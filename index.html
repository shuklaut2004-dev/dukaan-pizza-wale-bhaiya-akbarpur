Our story

A small counter by the overbridge that Akbarpur adopted as its own

I started Pizza Wale Bhaiya four years ago with one oven and a handwritten menu taped to the counter. My younger brother used to help me hand out orders after his college classes, and between the two of us we figured out the toppings people here actually wanted — a little spicier, a lot more cheese.

In the first few months, people would walk past thinking a shop this small couldn't make a pizza worth stopping for. Now our regulars call ahead before they've even parked their bike, because they know the dough's already resting fresh for the day.

— Ayush Shukla, Owner
